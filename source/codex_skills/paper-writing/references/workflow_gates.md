# Workflow and Gates

## Phase 0: Topic Selection

Answer three questions before drafting:

- Scope: what body of work is covered and excluded?
- Angle: what structural novelty does this paper add?
- Audience: which research community or venue should the writing satisfy?

Write the answers to `topic.yaml`.

For original research, also run `build_research_brief.py` and keep `research_brief.md` plus `story/00_research_brief.md` as the high-context source of truth.

## Phase 1: Draft, Iterations 1-6

Target score: 6.0.

- Iteration 1: structure skeleton, introduction, background, compile.
- Iteration 2: literature recall and LQS scoring.
- Iteration 3: core sections plus at least two figures/tables.
- Iteration 4: citation depth classification, venue upgrade, benchmarks/future/conclusion.
- Iteration 5: citation verification, compile, first review.
- Iteration 6: route review weaknesses, fix, compile, gate check, snapshot.

## Phase 2: Deep Improvement, Iterations 7-9

Target score: 7.5 to 8.0.

- Iteration 7: preregister, create `experiments/registry.json`, write `experiments/run_queue.md`, design, and execute a minimal experiment.
- Iteration 8: run `collect_paper_results.py`, run `generate_paper_figures.py`, turn real results into figures/tables, and integrate findings.
- Iteration 9: compile, review, route weaknesses, gate check.

## Phase 3: Sprint, Iterations 10+

Target score: 8.5 or higher.

Loop:

1. Review.
2. Route weaknesses.
3. Fix Major issues first.
4. Compile.
5. Gate check.
6. Snapshot.

Stop only when `next_iteration.py` reports a stop condition.

## Quality Gates

Literature:

- Draft: at least 80 references.
- Final: at least `target_pages * 3` references.
- Within-one-year references at least 40%.
- Accepted references at least 30%.
- arXiv-only references at most 60%.
- BibTeX verification rate at least 80%.
- Every taxonomy cell needs A/B references before final.

Experiment:

- `experiments/preregister.md` exists before results.
- Results link to a specific paper claim.
- Planned-only original research is allowed when data/GPU are unavailable, but it must have executable `experiments/todo_runs.md`, at least 2 standard datasets, 6 strong baselines, 4 ablations, 1 robustness study, and an efficiency item.
- Executed results have at least 3 trials.
- Executed results include p-value, confidence interval, or statistical summary.
- Executed results document ceiling/floor checks.

Structure:

- `main.tex` compiles with no errors and no undefined references.
- Each section `.tex` file has at most 300 lines.
- Conclusion exists and aligns with the abstract.
- Core sections include critical assessment.
- At least one formal claim is present.

Figures and tables:

- Draft: at least two figures/tables.
- Final long survey: at least 10 tables and 6 figures.
- Tables use `booktabs` and no vertical lines.
- Captions state findings.
- Every figure/table is referenced in text.

Research pipeline for original research:

- `research_brief.md` and `story/00_research_brief.md` exist and are substantial.
- `experiments/registry.json` contains at least E1 main, E2 ablation, and E3 robustness experiments.
- Every registry experiment has claim, purpose, datasets, baselines, metrics, commands, expected outputs, and paper targets.
- `experiments/run_queue.md` contains executable commands.
- `results_numbers.tex` is generated by `collect_paper_results.py`.
- `experiments/paper_numbers.json` records macro provenance and placeholder status.
- `figures/figure_manifest.json` records generated figures or a planned-only figure state.

Submission quality for original research:

- Compiled PDF reaches the project target page count, normally at least 7 pages for AAAI-style papers.
- Manuscript body has at least 5,500 words unless `state.json` explicitly overrides this threshold.
- Default section budgets: Introduction 900 words, Related Work 800, Method 1200, Experiments 1000, Analysis/Ablation 600, Limitations 150, Conclusion 120.
- At least 20 citation commands, with at least 3 in Introduction and 8 in Related Work.
- At least 3 equation blocks and 1 algorithm or pseudocode block.
- At least 4 figure mentions and 3 table mentions in the manuscript narrative.
- AAAI targets must use an AAAI style/package and an anonymous author marker.
- Severe layout overflow fails: more than 12 overfull hboxes or any overfull hbox above 25pt.

Final review:

- All prerequisite gates pass.
- Latest median review score reaches target.
- Regression check confirms previous fixes remain fixed.
